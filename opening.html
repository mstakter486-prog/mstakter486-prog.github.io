Opening & Notice

I am open to supervising students at Oakland University. However, at this moment I do not have funding to support any student. I am hopeful to receive funding to support students in the future.

Any motivated student who would like to do research with me is welcome — feel free to reach out to me at akter@oakland.edu.

Oakland University Students (Undergraduate / Master's / Ph.D.): I am happy to supervise your thesis or dissertation. I am looking for students who are hardworking, self-motivated, and eager to publish in the areas of Software Security and Natural Language Processing (NLP).

If you are an OU student interested in conducting research and publishing papers in these areas as part of your thesis or dissertation, please reach out — I would love to hear from you.

Contact me at akter@oakland.edu with your CV and a brief statement of your research interests.

Non-Oakland University students: if interested, you are welcome to reach out to collaborate on research without expecting any funding support from me at this moment. If I receive funding, I would be happy to recruit.

Feel free to contact me at akter@oakland.edu.
