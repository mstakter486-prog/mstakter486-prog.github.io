Opening & Notice

I am open to supervising students at Oakland University. However, at this moment I do not have funding to support any student. I am hopeful to receive funding to support students in the future.

Any motivated student who would like to do research with me is welcome — feel free to reach out to me at akter@oakland.edu.

Students from Oakland University will need to arrange their own funding.

Non-Oakland University students: if interested, you are welcome to reach out to collaborate on research without expecting any funding support from me.
